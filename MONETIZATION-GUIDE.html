Measure before spending

Use GA4 to inspect returning readers, engaged reading, useful stories and support-link clicks. Check AdSense itself for ad impressions, earnings and page RPM. Analytics opt-in means GA4 is not a complete census of visitors. After you have real data, estimate advertising income as monetized pageviews ÷ 1,000 × observed page RPM. No dependable earnings estimate can be made from the article count alone.

Keep costs low until those observations justify more work. Adding more ad networks at launch can add scripts and consent complexity without producing meaningful income. Publishing and payment automation can reduce administration; trustworthy content and audience growth still need attention.
