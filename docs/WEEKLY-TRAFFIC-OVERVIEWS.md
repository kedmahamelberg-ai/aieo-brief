# Weekly traffic overviews

The Brief publishes two source-linked weekly editorial products.

## The week from above

A sub-minute comparison of reporting discovered in China, the United States, the United Kingdom, France and Canada. It describes the emphasis of the selected complete-content reporting stream. It does not claim to represent a country, national policy, public opinion or all AI activity in that market.

## The research compass

A sub-minute synthesis of research displayed during the edition. Each listed paper keeps its original source link, authors, evidence stage and limits. University and research-institution names are published only when supplied by the source or matched in OpenAlex metadata. Missing affiliations are never guessed.

## Images

The river-delta and forest-path illustrations are original AIEO assets dedicated to the public domain under CC0. They are metaphors, not documentary photographs.

## Archive and social use

Each completed Observatory edition receives a stable page under `/week-from-above/`. Older editions remain in the archive. `data/social/current.json` contains two platform-ready starting drafts with exact links. A person or an authorised platform workflow must still approve and publish social posts.

## Traffic purpose

The feature creates an original, recurring reason to return and a page that can be shared by readers, researchers and journalists. It does not guarantee search ranking, press coverage, clicks or advertising income.
